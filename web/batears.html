🦇 Bat Ears test: ultrasonic Doppler hand gestures

The speaker plays a high tone you can barely hear, and the frequency change (Doppler) of the sound reflected back from your hand tells push from pull. Use the laptop’s built-in speakers and mic, not headphones or Bluetooth.

How to test with your hand
  1. ▶ Start → in the mic request below the address bar, choose Allow.
  2. For the first 1.5 s keep your hand away and still (measuring the baseline).
  3. "Reflected signal SNR" should be 20 dB or more. If lower, raise the volume or lower the frequency to 19 kHz or 18 kHz.
  4. 10–20 cm in front of the screen, quickly push your palm toward the screen 5 times, rest a few seconds, then pull 5 times.
  5. Keep your hand away and still for 10 s, and check that the numbers don’t rise on their own.
  6. Band check once, then Copy results → paste into Discord.
