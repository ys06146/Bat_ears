🎤 Microphone

After turning it on, keep your hand away and wait 1.5 s. Hand about 20 cm from the screen. To just see the screen without a mic, use the keyboard: ↑ push · ↓ pull · W wave.

📝 Enroll

Pick 3–6 gestures in an order and repeat them in your own rhythm. The first one is practice and isn’t recorded. The more even your repetitions, the tighter the lock.

🔓 Unlock

Repeat the order and rhythm exactly. It decides 0.8 s after the last gesture.

📁 Evaluation log

Each attempt records the lock owner, performer, owner/other, order match and style score (for FAR and FRR, python ml/auth_report.py).

Uses the data folder picked on the main page (Drive nhng/ml/data).
