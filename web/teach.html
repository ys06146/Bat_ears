1. Setup

📋 Phone-overhead experiment (experiment, effect unconfirmed: in the last two sessions this phone’s path didn’t follow the hand. Layout: 📱A 17000 at 45 cm above the hand spot, facing down; 📱B off)
  • Set the screen almost upright (about 95°). Tilted far back, the webcam can’t see a hand over the keyboard
  • Move your hand 25–45 cm from the webcam and 18–25 cm above the desk (the 📏 distance on the right should be green). Keep the hand centered in the preview
  • Use the laptop speakers and mic (no earphones), volume 50% or more. On the phones, start the tone with tone.html and point them at the laptop
  • 📹 The raw video (saved by default) stays only in this computer’s data folder (ml/data/teach) and never goes to git or the public repo. Turn it off to save only the hand joint coordinates (numbers)
