    How to use
    1. Use the laptop’s built-in speakers and mic (no headphones or Bluetooth), volume 50% or more.
    2. ▶ Start → allow the mic → keep your hand away for 1.5 s (baseline).
    3. 10–20 cm in front of the screen, push your palm toward the screen to send a meteor flying. Pull to grab a star. Hit several quickly in a row for a combo multiplier.
    4. Wave your hand quickly back and forth for a shockwave (destroys every meteor on screen, 8 s recharge).
    5. 'Left/right swipe' switched on, sweep your hand left or right to slice meteors on that side (the laptop needs speakers on both sides).
    6. To just watch the game without a mic, use the keyboard: ↑ (push), ↓ (pull), ← → (swipe), W (wave), M (mute sound effects). After touching a slider, click an empty spot once.
